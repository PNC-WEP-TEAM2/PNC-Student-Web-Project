Our story

We build better, together.

Synergy is a five-member student project team built around curiosity, craft, and the belief that shared ownership makes better work.

Different strengths. One clear direction.

How we show up

Our team brings together developers, designers, strategists, and storytellers. We work in the open, give feedback generously, and keep the goal visible when the details get complicated.

From the first sketch to the final polish, every person has a meaningful hand in what we make. That is the kind of teamwork we want to practice and carry forward.
